## No medical advice

Supplement Lab is a tool for **self-observation**. It does not make diagnoses, does not give
treatment recommendations and does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist. The analyses
are based solely on your own subjective information and are not scientific studies.
Connections (“patterns”) are indications, not proof.

- **Never stop taking prescription medication on your own** or change its dose.
- Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before taking new dietary supplements –
  especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have pre-existing conditions, take medication or are under 18.
- If you have severe or persistent symptoms: stop taking it and have it checked by a doctor.
- The usual doses shown are general guide values, not a personal
  recommendation. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions.

## Content

Information on supplements, facts and tips has been compiled carefully, but may be
incomplete or change as new findings emerge. Community values show the experiences of
other users and are not representative.

## Purchases and Lab Pro

The core features are free. In the store apps, “Lab Pro” is available as a subscription (monthly or yearly) or as a
one-time purchase (“lifetime”). There are currently no purchases in the web app.

- **Processing:** purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and charged to your Apple ID or
  Google Play account; their terms also apply. Prices are shown in the app before purchase (in your local currency,
  as provided by the store).
- **Free week (yearly plan only):** the yearly plan may come with a free trial week – only once and only if you have
  not used it in the respective store before. After that, the yearly price is charged unless you cancel beforehand.
  Cancel during the free week and you won’t be charged.
- **Renewal:** subscriptions renew automatically for the same period at the same price unless cancelled at least
  24 hours before the end of the current period; renewal is charged within the 24 hours before it ends.
- **Cancelling:** at any time in your store account settings – iPhone: “Settings → your name → Subscriptions”;
  Android: “Google Play app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions”. Deleting the app does not cancel a
  subscription. Periods you have already paid for remain usable until they end.
- **Lifetime:** one-time payment, no subscription, no renewal. If you already have a subscription, you need to cancel
  it yourself; the store does not end it automatically.
- **Restore:** “Restore purchases” brings purchased subscriptions and purchases to a new device with the same Apple ID
  or Google account.
- **Refunds** follow Apple’s or Google’s rules and are requested there.
- **Founders:** anyone who starts using the app on or before 30 November 2026 gets Lab Pro permanently at no cost.
  Because there is no account, founder status is tied to the app data on your device; it cannot be transferred via
  “Restore purchases”.
