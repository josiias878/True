## No medical advice

Supplement Lab is a tool for **self-observation**. It does not make diagnoses, does not give
treatment recommendations and does not replace advice from a doctor or pharmacist. The analyses
are based solely on your own subjective information and are not scientific studies.
Connections (“patterns”) are indications, not proof.

- **Never stop taking prescription medication on your own** or change its dose.
- Talk to your doctor or pharmacist before taking new dietary supplements –
  especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have pre-existing conditions, take medication or are under 18.
- If you have severe or persistent symptoms: stop taking it and have it checked by a doctor.
- The usual doses shown are general guide values, not a personal
  recommendation. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions.

## Content

Information on supplements, facts and tips has been compiled carefully, but may be
incomplete or change as new findings emerge. Community values show the experiences of
other users and are not representative.
